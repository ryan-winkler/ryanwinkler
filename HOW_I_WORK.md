# How I work

Short, operational behaviours that guide my day to day work.

## Signals and decisions

- Everything is a signal. Conversations, incidents, and edge cases count.
- I treat signal quality as a product problem.
- I write decisions down, even when they feel obvious.

## Ownership and boundaries

- I clarify system boundaries early.
- I prefer explicit ownership over shared responsibility.
- I keep language consistent across teams and tools.

## Feedback loops

- I set feedback loops before I ship.
- I prefer evidence over opinion in reviews.
- I document what changed and why.

## Operating rhythm

- I use runbooks for repeatable work.
- I keep meetings for coordination, not discovery.
- I prioritise written updates and async review.
