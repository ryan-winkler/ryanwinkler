# Bookmarks I Still Recommend

I cut this list down on purpose. Everything here is live, worth following, and tied to the kind of product work this repo is trying to be useful for.

## When I need the operating system itself

- [Get Shit Done](https://github.com/glittercowboy/get-shit-done) — A spec-first delivery system for turning messy work into clear phases. Useful when the problem is real, cross-functional, and easy to hand-wave.
- [Operator OS](https://github.com/vsauter/operator-os) — A good reference point for making agent-heavy personal systems legible. Worth reading when you want to compare how other people structure operator workflows.
- [DomainDrivenDesign](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/DomainDrivenDesign.html) — Still the shortest good reminder that boundaries and language are product work too.
- [KCS v6 Methodology](https://www.serviceinnovation.org/kcs/) — The anchor I come back to when I want knowledge capture to happen in the flow of work instead of after the fact.

## When product decisions are getting messy

- [RICE: Simple Prioritization for Product Managers](https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/) — Still the clearest quick explanation of RICE. Good when a prioritization conversation is getting vague.
- [Introducing DRICE](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/introducing-drice-a-modern-prioritization) — A better fit than plain RICE when dependencies and risk start to dominate the work.
- [The Four Big Risks](https://svpg.com/four-big-risks/) — A short, useful reminder of where product bets usually go wrong.

## When I want AI product work to stay grounded

- [Applied ML](https://github.com/eugeneyan/applied-ml) — A strong reading shelf for production ML and evaluation. Useful when you want something deeper than launch theatre.
- [Moltis](https://www.moltis.org/) — The live gateway I use now. Worth following if you care about provider routing and MCP-style integration without unnecessary complexity.
- [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) — Still one of the most practical ways to keep local model work close to day-to-day experimentation.
- [Open Interpreter](https://github.com/OpenInterpreter/open-interpreter) — Worth keeping an eye on if you care about computer-use agents and where those interfaces start to get messy.

## When trust and reliability matter more than feature volume

- [Integrity Institute](https://integrityinstitute.org/) — Useful when trust and safety questions need sharper language and better framing.
- [Google SRE Books](https://sre.google/books/) — Still one of the best reliability references that product managers should actually read.
