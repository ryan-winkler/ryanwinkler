# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in this repository! Contributions, suggestions, and discussions are always welcome.

## How to Contribute

### Found a Typo or Error?

Please open a pull request! I appreciate help keeping this content accurate and clear.

### Have a Suggestion?

Open an issue or pull request with:

- What you'd like to see added or changed
- Why it would be valuable
- Any examples or references

### Want to Discuss an Approach?

I'd love to hear your thoughts! Open an issue to:

- Share alternative approaches to frameworks or processes
- Discuss product management practices
- Ask questions about how something works

### Improving Templates or Runbooks?

If you have improvements to templates, runbooks, or workflows:

1. Fork the repository
2. Make your changes
3. Open a pull request with a description of the improvement

I'll review and merge helpful contributions.

## What I'm Looking For

**Helpful contributions**:

- Typo fixes and clarity improvements
- Additional examples or use cases
- Better explanations of frameworks
- New templates or runbooks that would be useful
- Links to relevant resources

**Great discussions**:

- Alternative approaches to product problems
- Questions about frameworks or processes
- Suggestions for new content
- Feedback on what's working or not working
