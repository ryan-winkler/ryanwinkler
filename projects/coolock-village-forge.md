# Coolock Village Forge

## Summary

Coolock Village Forge is a community-led civic coordination site for Coolock Village in Dublin.

It combines community information, planning notices, local business content, and practical public-service style publishing in one place.

## The product problem

Local information is usually fragmented, stale, or hard to trust.

The job here is not to build a giant platform. It is to make a small public system useful:

- information should be easy to find
- pages should be understandable on first read
- structured content and SEO should help the right people find the right page
- the site should stay maintainable for a small team

## Constraints that matter

- small-team publishing and maintenance
- civic and community trust
- accessibility and structured content
- strong search behavior without heavy product overhead
- Astro, Cloudflare, and DDD/KCS discipline instead of ad hoc sprawl

## Why it matters now

- It is a live example of product work where clarity matters more than feature volume
- It keeps search, structured content, accessibility, and trust tied to an actual audience
- It shows how I work when content, technical constraints, and public usefulness all have to stay aligned

## Current lessons

- public trust is usually won through clarity and consistency, not feature count
- structured content is one of the most practical product levers on a small site
- small community systems still benefit from bounded contexts and explicit operating rules
